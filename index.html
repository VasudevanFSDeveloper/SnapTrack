Legal & Compliance Hub

1. Introduction & Privacy Commitment

SnapTrack Technologies Private Limited ("SnapTrack", "we", "us") values your fundamental right to privacy. This policy explains our local-first financial ledger storage model and compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP 2023).

2. Zero-Telemetry Architecture

SnapTrack operates on a strictly client-side local architecture. Your financial entries, balances, and debtor information are stored in your device's browser sandbox (localStorage) and are never transmitted to, stored on, or monetized via external backend servers.

3. Your Data Subject Rights

You have full legal rights over your data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Export your full dataset at any time in standard JSON or CSV formats.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit or modify any income or expense entry instantly in the app interface.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Permanently wipe all stored records using the "Erase Local Records" button.

4. Contact the Data Protection Officer

For inquiries, email dpo@snaptrack.app.
